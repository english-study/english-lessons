    A phone call about a new flat

    Two friends, A and B. A has a new flat. Two lines below have a mistake — can you hear or read which ones?

    A

    Hi! Guess what — I have a new flat now!

    B

    Wow! How is it?

    A

    It's small, but really cosy. There's a lovely view from the kitchen — you can see the whole street.

    B

    Nice! What's the living room like?

    A

    There's a soft rug in the middle, and there is two big windows, so it's really bright.

    B

    Sounds nice. What about the bedroom?

    A

    That's the problem — there isn't a wardrobe in the bedroom, only two drawers under the bed.

    B

    Oh no. Is there a mirror, at least?

    A

    Yes, there are a mirror in the hall, actually a really big one.

    B

    Good. And what about the ceiling — is it high?

    A

    Yes, it's quite high, so the room feels very big. There's a small balcony too.

    B

    Perfect for coffee in the morning! Does the landlord know about the wardrobe?

    A

    Not yet — maybe soon.

    Grammar

    There is / there are

    Part 1 — From the text

    4 sentences from what you just read: 2 have a mistake, 2 are correct.

    The verb here doesn't agree with "there" — it agrees with the noun right after it. That's the opposite of normal word order, where the subject comes first and decides the verb: here the "subject" comes after, so you look ahead (singular or plural?) before you choose is/are.
